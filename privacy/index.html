Warehouse WMS Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Warehouse WMS helps authorized warehouse teams manage inventory, product photos, barcodes, quantities, locations, and movement history.

Information handled by the app

  • Inventory records, product descriptions, quantities, locations, barcodes, movement history, and product photos entered by authorized users.
  • An email address when cloud authentication is enabled.
  • Camera and photo-library access when a user scans a barcode or chooses a product photo.
  • Device authentication results are handled by the operating system. The app does not receive or store biometric data.

Inventory data is stored on the device. When cloud synchronization is enabled, authorized inventory data and product photos may also be stored using Google Firebase so approved devices can stay synchronized.

Advertising

Warehouse WMS uses Google AdMob to display advertising on Android and iOS. Google and its advertising partners may process device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions, and consent choices to provide, measure, and protect advertising. Where required, the app asks for advertising consent before requesting ads. Users can review their choices from Ad Privacy Choices in the app.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: Google partner sites policy.

Data sharing

Warehouse WMS does not publish inventory records or product photos. Data is shared only with service providers needed to operate enabled features, such as Firebase for authentication and synchronization and Google AdMob for advertising, subject to their privacy terms.

Data retention and deletion

Local inventory remains on a device until an authorized user removes it or the app's data is erased. Cloud data remains until it is deleted by an authorized administrator. Advertising consent choices can be changed from the app.

Children

Warehouse WMS is a business inventory tool and is not directed to children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's features or service providers change. The latest version will remain available at this address.

Contact
